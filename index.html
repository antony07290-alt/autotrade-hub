How the signal rule works

  • Waits for 1,000 ticks and compares two separate 500-tick windows.
  • Looks for the same Over/Under barrier to show an observed frequency at least 5 percentage points above its theoretical baseline in both windows.
  • If no barrier passes, it displays WAIT — no entry.
  • Even a qualifying result can be random and may stop working. Test on demo first; never treat it as guaranteed profit.
